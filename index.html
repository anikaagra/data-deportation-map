Context

Texas alone accounts for 48% of all U.S. deportations, mainly due to its 1,200-mile border shared with Mexico. Following with a large gap, California and Arizona reflect the southwestern enforcement corridor. Interior states like Illinois and New York show the reach of Secure Communities (2008–2014) and later 287(g) agreements, which expanded federal immigration enforcement into local communities.
